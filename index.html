01 / About

Researching the future of mobility.

My work connects transportation engineering, operations research, energy systems, and data analytics.

I am a Ph.D. student in Civil and Environmental Engineering at the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign.

My research focuses on the design and operation of transportation and energy systems under uncertainty. I develop mathematical models, computational algorithms, and data-driven methods for electric vehicle charging, traveler behavior, network resilience, and infrastructure planning.

My broader objective is to understand how emerging mobility technologies can support transportation systems that are more efficient, reliable, equitable, and resilient.

Core research directions.

Interdisciplinary research at the intersection of mobility, energy, uncertainty, and infrastructure.

01

Transportation Network Modeling

Modeling traveler behavior, congestion, route choice, and network performance under uncertain and disrupted transportation conditions.

02

Electric Vehicle Charging

Studying charging behavior, charging infrastructure, fleet electrification, and interactions between transportation and electric power systems.

03

Optimization Under Uncertainty

Developing stochastic, robust, distributionally robust, and risk-aware optimization approaches for complex infrastructure systems.

04

Infrastructure Resilience

Evaluating how transportation and charging systems respond to extreme weather, disruptions, uncertain demand, and infrastructure failures.
