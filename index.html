About

I am a Ph.D. student in Civil and Environmental Engineering at the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign. My work lies at the intersection of transportation engineering, operations research, electric mobility, and data analytics.

I am particularly interested in how uncertainty, traveler behavior, charging demand, infrastructure disruptions, and decentralized decision-making affect the performance of transportation and energy systems.
